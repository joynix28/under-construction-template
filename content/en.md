## Features

- **Protection & anti-raid**: AI AutoModeration, anti-raid, anti-bot, anti-ghostping, anti-spam, automatic warnings.
- **Moderation**: ban, kick, warnings (`/warn`, `/warnings`, `/unwarn`), jail (`/jail`).
- **Levels & XP**: text and voice leveling, rank cards, automatic role rewards, leaderboards.
- **Economy**: currency, shop, progression (`/argent`, `/admin-xp`).
- **Unlimited AI**: chat, image generation, voice-note summaries, web search (`/invoquer-ia`, `/toggle-ai`).
- **Management**: tickets (`/tickets`), recruitment, auto-roles, role menus (`/roles-menu`), logs and statistics.
- **Community**: giveaways, polls, confessions, reviews, partnerships.
- **Entertainment**: built-in games (chess, checkers, connect four), music and radio (`/radio`).
- **Dashboard**: full configuration from the web, without typing commands.
- **Info & GDPR**: `/bot`, `/stats`, `/infos`, `/help`, `/dashboard`, `/mes-donnees`.
