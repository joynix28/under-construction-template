FAQ

Everything about Chell

Is Chell really free? +
Yes. Chell is 100% free and unlimited, including its AI module. There is no credit to buy, no subscription and no feature locked behind a paid tier.
How do I add Chell to my Discord server? +
Click “Add to Discord”, pick your server, then authorise the requested permissions. Installation takes under a minute; configuration is done from the web dashboard.
What features does Chell offer? +
AI moderation (AutoMod), anti-raid and anti-ghostping, levels and XP, economy and shop, tickets and recruitment, auto-roles and menus, giveaways, polls and confessions, logs, statistics, games, music and radio — all configurable from a web dashboard.
How many commands does Chell have? +
103 commands: 94 slash commands and 9 context menus, across 10 categories. The full reference is on the dashboard.
Is Chell verified by Discord? +
Yes. Chell is certified and listed in the official Discord application directory. It is also hand-verified on the French bot list Botillon.
Is the bot in French or English? +
Chell is a French-speaking bot: its commands and messages are in French, making it a great fit for French-speaking communities. This English page presents it for international discovery.
Who created Chell? +
Chell was created by joynix28 (founder and owner), with thomas0535 as developer and co-founder and feulio as co-founder and administrator.
Can I configure Chell without typing commands? +
Yes. Everything is configured from the web dashboard: welcomes, moderation, tickets, levels, logs and more, without writing a single command.
How many servers is Chell on? +
Chell is installed on more than 2,000 Discord servers with over 147,000 users. The server count is updated live on this page from the official Discord directory.
